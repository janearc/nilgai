# firang: candidates

One folder per candidate, named by its Gaia DR3 source_id. Each holds
what is known of the star and where it stands, and its spectra and
their reading when there are any.

A star is a candidate when its path comes from Sgr A* across its
measurement errors and it moves fast and outward through the galaxy
now. In the first pass that meant over 400 km/s, with at least a fifth
of its draws passing within 100 pc of Sgr A*.

Seven stars met that rule. These five, with radial velocities well
measured, are here as the first pass found them, unreviewed:

- [4037334576111139712](4037334576111139712/README.md)
- [4385671099714045824](4385671099714045824/README.md)
- [6736906690592556672](6736906690592556672/README.md)
- [4081136684118965760](4081136684118965760/README.md)
- [6760416924931944832](6760416924931944832/README.md)

The other two, 4094201527955913856 and 4066702325525092736, are set
aside. They are faint, with radial velocities near -680 km/s, and are
suspect for the reason [sixteen.md](../sixteen.md) gives.
