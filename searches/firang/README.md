# firang

Firang means foreigners: stars that came from the galactic centre and
live out here now.

Did planets form around Sgr A*, and can we see them in the stars it
has thrown out? When Sgr A* was active, planets, blanets, may have formed
in its dusty disc, and stars near the centre would swallow some.

Stars thrown out since then are out of the dust, so their surfaces
still show what they ate: more of the refractory elements against the
volatile ones, and more lithium, than stars like them. Hot stars blur
the reading, since diffusion changes their surfaces; cool stars test it.

Its stages are in [stages.md](stages.md): orbit traceback selects
candidates, injection-recovery tests the selection, and spectroscopic
follow-up reads their chemistry. cmd/firang is the tool that runs them.

Where it stands is in [first-pass.md](first-pass.md): no star is yet
shown to have come from Sgr A*. Why it might work, and where it is
weakest, is in [the synthesis brief](reports/full-synthesis-brief.md),
from the papers in sources/.

Run it here: [.game](.game) has the targets, numbers.yaml the numbers,
data/ the output, and [candidates](candidates/README.md) a folder a star.
